# What is Shirimono?

Right now, I am fully focused on a new project: Shirimono. It is an application designed to help Japanese language students learn efficiently and playfully.

I used to be a Japanese student myself (I still am to some extent, but not as actively), and I had to go through a ton of different study methods: various apps, textbooks for the JLPT, etc.
However, the method that really helped me progress was the SRS (Spaced Repetition System). Basically, this method consists of periodic review sessions on the same learning points until they are firmly consolidated in your memory.

# How is Shirimono different?

I used Wanikani, which was really good for learning Kanji and some vocabulary. So, I took inspiration from it to create my own application that not only covers Kanji but also vocabulary *and* grammar points.
It is exactly the app I wish I had used a few years ago.

I haven't found any other app that allows you to do the same thing all in one place yet, although Bunpro comes very close.

# What's next?

The web version is already available at shirimono.fun. I'm doing my best to develop a high-quality application.
As for the mobile version, development will start in February (probably using React Native).

Just a little more patience, it's coming very soon!
See you soon!
