So... I've been asking myself the question for a few months now: should I go back to France?
I've been living in Tokyo since 2018 and I love my life here.

Japan is a beautiful country, very clean, and the residents have a top-tier sense of civic duty. I've never had any problems here, never been a victim of theft or crime.

It's so safe that children can walk home alone after school starting from kindergarten age.

But anyway, enough with the good sides of Japan (the list is long), there are also a few points that make me think about returning to France.

## Education

I am currently married, and my wife and I are thinking about having children soon. I think a lot about how I'm going to raise them. And I think France would be the place that suits me best.

Well, first of all, I really want my children to speak French. My wife and I speak Japanese (she doesn't speak French at all for now). Consequently, speaking only Japanese at home and letting them speak French at school doesn't seem like a bad idea.

Conversely, if we decided to stay in Japan, I think the kids would speak Japanese at home and at school, and even if I give it my all to teach them French at home, I'm not sure they would manage it. The French international school is very expensive, so that's not an option.

Next, maybe I'm being a bit too pessimistic and influenced by stereotypes, but I feel like Japanese children don't have the same concept of family as in the West.
Most Japanese people I know here aren't very close to their families. Even if they live just a few minutes away, they aren't always in contact with them.

I wonder if it's because adults, but also children, spend a lot of time doing their own things: adults work a lot and children are often in after-school activities like *bukatsu* (club activities) or cram school.

## Grandparents

Following the same reasoning, if I stayed in Japan, because my wife's parents live in Saga prefecture at the other end of the country, my children wouldn't see their grandparents regularly. In France, I'm sure my parents would see my children regularly and help us raise them.

## Work

Well, nothing new here. In Japan, people work a lot. I mean... in my case, it's not that terrible. I work remotely and my colleagues are really nice. My company is considered a "chill" company by Japanese standards.

Nevertheless, I am still subject to the same labor laws as everyone else. That means 9 hours of work per day (usually, we do more), and 10 days of paid leave per year.

If I returned to France, I could probably find a job that would allow me to work fewer hours and spend more time with my family or on my activities. On the other hand, the salary might be lower than what I earn here.

## Climate and Natural Disasters

Well, in Japan, summer is a heatwave every day. And it's going to get worse year by year due to climate change. That's a small argument in favor of France. Summers in France are more pleasant, but above all, the days are longer.

Even in summer, the sun sets around 7 PM here, but well, it's still dark after work. Because of that, I feel like the days go by faster and become more monotonous.

Finally, natural disasters. In Japan, it's not rare to see floods, typhoons, earthquakes... Apparently, the "Big One" is coming soon, and that's not good news. But while living in *tower mansions* (high-rise condos) in Tokyo makes you dream a bit, the idea of finding myself stuck on the 30th floor of a building during an earthquake scares the hell out of me.

Anyway, there you go. These are a few thoughts coming to mind. I don't know if I'm going to return to France, but I think I'm going to reflect on it a bit more. I'll try to list other arguments for both sides in other posts.
