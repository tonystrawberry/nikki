# Day one: why this diary?

Today marks the beginning of something new. I've decided to create this digital diary — a space where I can document my life, thoughts, and reflections.

## Why a diary?

In our hyper-connected world, we share a lot on social media. But those shares are often superficial, formatted to please an algorithm. I wanted something different.

> "A diary is a mirror where one looks at oneself without complacency."

This diary will be:

- **Personal** — My real thoughts, not a version edited for likes
- **Varied** — From technology to philosophy, including movies and daily life
- **Authentic** — My doubts, my joys, my discoveries

## What you'll find here

I plan to write about everything that moves me:

- **Reflections** — My worldview, existential questions
- **Experiences** — Travels, encounters, memorable moments
- **Culture** — Movies, series, music, books that left a mark
- **Work** — My career, projects, learnings
- **Daily life** — The little things that make life

## A new chapter

This first day of 2026 feels perfect to start. A new year, a new project, a new way to reflect on my life.

If you're reading these lines, welcome to my world. 🙏
