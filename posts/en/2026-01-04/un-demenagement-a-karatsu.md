The owner of the house where we currently live is returning to Japan in February. She was forced to quit her job in America and couldn't renew her visa.

So, it's an emergency situation, she's coming back to Japan and we have to vacate our house in February.
After discussing it with Fumika, we thought that Karatsu would be a good place to live for a while before a potential return to France.

I really like Karatsu. It's a small coastal town located in Saga prefecture. Close to the sea and the mountains.

There are nice spots for walks: around Karatsu Castle, the pine forest, the beach, and Itoshima just a few minutes away by car.

I already go there every year to visit my in-laws who live there, and it's always a pleasure.
Fumika's parents are always very welcoming, and I always feel at ease there.

Also, Baba-chan is now in a nursing home (wait for it... with an ocean view!) and it's really nice to be able to see her regularly.
I think she must feel a bit lonely in that nursing home. The last time we visited her, she said she wanted to go home.

Well, before that... we have our work cut out for us. We have to sell or pack all the stuff we want to bring to Karatsu, including large furniture and appliances.

In any case, I can't wait. I've been wanting to experience living outside the hustle and bustle of Tokyo.
