# Visit to some very dear friends

Today, I visited friends and their families.

First, Sango and his family. Sango is a friend I met during my exchange semester at the University of Tsukuba. He is a guy with a heart of gold. He has started a wonderful family with his wife and his two daughters, who are both super cute (I mean, really...).

We made crepes together for a morning snack. A big success! Sango also gave me some good advice, like a big brother would to his little brother, regarding my relationship with Fumika (it's not all sunshine and rainbows at the moment).
Then, a little McDonald's for lunch. We ate in the common area of his residence.

His residence, by the way, is the perfect place to raise children. There are so many families living there that it becomes very easy to find friends for the kids.
Great morning!

After that, I went to see Ouss', a very dear friend I met at Monstarlab, who is also the cream of the crop.
His daughter is now a year and a few months old. The last time I saw her, she had the same hairstyle as her dad, but her hair has grown since then! Way too cute. But she was very scared of me. Apparently, she prefers female presence.

I talked about everything and nothing with Ouss'. It is always a pleasure to see him.
I am leaving for Karatsu very soon, but I would like to see them one last time before I go...
