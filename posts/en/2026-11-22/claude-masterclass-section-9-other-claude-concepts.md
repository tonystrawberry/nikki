## TL;DR

Claude now lives **inside Office** (both add-ins were in beta when the course was recorded), the Chrome extension organises its work in **tab groups**, and you can bring your **ChatGPT memory** over in a couple of minutes.

## Claude in PowerPoint

- Install it from claude.com → *Meet Claude* → **Claude for PowerPoint** (Microsoft Store), then open **Add-ins → Claude** and sign in.
- It can build whole decks, add slides in the deck's existing theme and fonts, or edit **only the shapes you select** (hold Shift to select several).
- The side panel has skills, connectors, web search and an "ask before editing" mode. It checks its own visual work, for example fixing spacing.

## Claude in Excel

- Install it the same way, or through Add-ins → *Get add-ins* → "Claude by Anthropic".
- Uses from the course: debug a broken sheet, financial modelling ("add an annual summary and highlight the balloon payment in year 5"), **extract financial statements from a PDF annual report**, and build dashboards and charts.
- In beta, if it misbehaves, sign out and back in or reinstall it. Save your file before big changes.

## Skills inside Office

Click **+** to use your Cowork skills there, or the built-in ones: `/deck-refresh` updates a deck with new numbers, and there's an investment-banking deck QA skill. Add your own under *Manage skills*, for example "apply my company's visual identity to any PowerPoint".

## Chrome tab groups and the extension

Every new Claude-in-Chrome chat creates its own **tab group**, and Claude controls only the tabs in that group. A group works like a project folder: collapse it, close it, and reopen the whole task in one click.

## Moving from ChatGPT

1. In Claude, go to **Settings → Capabilities → Import memory from other AI providers** and copy the prompt.
2. In ChatGPT, turn on **Thinking** mode and make sure the three *reference saved memories / chat history* settings are on, then paste the prompt.
3. Paste ChatGPT's answer back into Claude and click **Add to memory**.

## Underused features

- **Several models in one request**: "use Sonnet for the web research, then Opus for the PDF summary" saves tokens.
- **Interactive diagrams** right in chat ("explain neural networks with a diagram"), downloadable as HTML.
- **Folder instructions** in Cowork projects, and selecting **several folders** at once.
- **Scheduled tasks** ("run this every day at 11 am"), **global instructions**, and memory.
