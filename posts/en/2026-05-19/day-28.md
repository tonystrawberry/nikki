- created my own Rails OpenAPI doc generator gem ([rails-openapi-generator](https://github.com/tonystrawberry/rails-openapi-generator)) for generating complete OpenAPI documentation from source code, with particular integration with the rails_param library
- used spec-kit to implement the gem — it's very simple to use, and having specifications makes the AI very precise about what to do
