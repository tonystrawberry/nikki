# Je m'appelais Franck

This is the autobiography of Vincent Lagaf', the guy who hosted the show "Le Bigdil" on TF1. I think.
He really livened up my childhood evenings after school. We often ate our family dinner while watching it.

I discovered a different character. He is very endearing, doesn't take himself too seriously, but above all, behind his funny demeanor, he is a very kind and authentic guy. He is the type to live life to the fullest.

That is what led him to have a life so rich in emotion and adventure, I suppose. He inspires me a lot. I would like to have a life just as exciting, but I feel like I "calculate" my actions and decisions too much. My life has become a bit too monotonous.

In any case, I really enjoyed reading this book!
